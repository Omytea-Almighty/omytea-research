## 1. When it runs (a departure from the schedule in the E2 design)

- The E2 design says "run after the v1.1 confirmatory phase and E1 have finished". This is changed, on instruction [omitted: organisation or decision-maker reference], to **start immediately**: concurrently with the confirmatory phase, using 2 processes with 1 thread per process.
- This affects only run time, not results:
  - the data and the initialisation of every run are determined by fixed seeds;
  - a different number of threads causes only tiny differences in results, at the level of floating-point summation order.
- The confirmatory-phase jobs are not affected: their results are determined by their own seeds and do not depend on machine load.
- E1 still runs after the confirmatory phase.

## 2. New arms B2 and B3 (same decision rule as B; descriptive only)

| Arm | Data | Step | Points | Source |
|---|---|---|---:|---|
| B2 | Household appliance electricity use (UCI "Appliances energy prediction", column `Appliances`) | 10 minutes | 19,735 | This repository (see the translator's note) |
| B3 | New York City taxi demand | 30 minutes | 10,320 | This repository (see the translator's note) |

[Translator's note: "This repository" means the private development repository. There, both CSV files lie outside the experiment directory and are git-ignored. Both are included unchanged in this supplement, as `repro/data/exp_e9/energydata_complete.csv` and `repro/data/exp_e3/nyc_taxi.csv`, with their licences and attributions in `repro/data/README.md`. B2 is the UCI "Appliances energy prediction" dataset (`energydata_complete.csv`); B3 is the New York City taxi series as packaged in the Numenta Anomaly Benchmark (NAB), file `data/realKnownCause/nyc_taxi.csv`. The published code (`src/osw/tasks_series.py`) reads them from `data/exp_e9/energydata_complete.csv` and `data/exp_e3/nyc_taxi.csv` under the supplement's `repro/` directory.]

**Processing:**
- Split by time: first 60% training, next 10% validation, last 30% test.
- Quintile thresholds are computed from the training part only; category = the number of thresholds strictly below the value.
- Windows are 26 context steps plus 8 forecast steps; they do not cross split boundaries; stride 1; every window is used.
- Seeds change only the initialisation and the batch order; the data split is fixed.

[Translator's note: DEVIATIONS.md D14(f) records that in the implementation the HMM was fitted only on the first 5,000 training windows, which cover the earliest 42% (B2) and 81% (B3) of the training period; the other models used every window (technical report §3.8).]

**Known characteristics (reported as they are, not corrected):**
- B2 has many repeated values; the frequencies of the five categories in the test part are 0.070 / 0.225 / 0.322 / 0.227 / 0.157, which is not uniform.
- Fraction of pairs of points 1 step apart that fall in the same category: B2 0.67, B3 0.73. 8 steps apart: B2 0.50, B3 0.25; B3 has a clear daily cycle.

**Metrics and comparison:** the same as arm B.
- The primary metric is the RPSS over steps 3–6, with the uniform climatology as reference.
- Budgets {150, 500}; decision rule Δ ≥ 0.10 and CI > 0.
- 3 seeds per configuration.
